# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Danish homeowners — both first-time sellers and experienced sellers — who are evaluating or about to list their property. They arrive with anxiety: "Am I choosing the wrong agent and leaving money on the table?" They are not real estate experts; they need the site to make the decision feel safe, simple, and free of risk. Context: desktop and mobile, often researching in the evening.

## Product Purpose

FindEjendomsmægler.nu is a free lead-matching service that connects Danish homeowners with the 3 best local real estate agents for their property. Success means a user submitting the form within the first visit. The value proposition: you get competitive offers, zero cost, zero obligation.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, Danish. Like a knowledgeable neighbor who has sold a lot of homes — warm, direct, no-nonsense. Not corporate, not flashy. Confident enough that it doesn't need to shout.

## Anti-references

- boliga.dk / boligsiden.dk — cluttered portal UI, listings-directory feel, no clear hierarchy
- Generic SaaS lead-gen — cold, feature-dump, startup-ish aesthetics
- High-pressure sales sites — scarcity manipulation, alarm colors, aggressive urgency

## Design Principles

1. **Earn trust before asking** — every piece of social proof, stat, and copy should reduce anxiety before the CTA appears
2. **One job, one screen** — the form flow is a single decision at a time; never overwhelm with parallel choices
3. **Danish directness** — clear, plain language; say what you mean; no euphemism or puffery
4. **Free and frictionless** — the zero-cost, zero-obligation message must be impossible to miss at every touchpoint
5. **Local credibility** — the map, agent count, and brand logos are there to prove real geographic presence, not just decoration

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. `prefers-reduced-motion` is implemented. Forms have ARIA labels. No specific known user needs beyond baseline Danish language support.
